Royal Encyclopedia & Tactical Guide

🏰 Kingdom Towers & Tier 4 Specializations

🏹 Archer Tower (Cost: 70g)

Rapid physical attacks against both Ground & Flying enemies. Weak against high Physical Armor.

• Rangers Hideout (T4-A): Blinding attack speed + Poison Arrows (True damage over time).
• Musketeer Garrison (T4-B): Extreme range sniper rifles + periodic Headshot Snipe.
🛡️ Militia Barracks (Cost: 75g)

Trains 3 Melee Soldiers stationed at a customizable Rally Point to physically block and stall ground foes.

• Holy Paladins (T4-A): Heavy armor tanks with Holy Light self-healing & Shield Strike.
• Barbarian Hall (T4-B): High-damage berserkers who also hurl axes at flying enemies!
🔮 Mage Guild (Cost: 100g)

Fires arcane bolts that ignore Physical Armor. Hits Ground & Flying enemies. Weak vs Magic Resistance.

• Arcane Wizard (T4-A): High-damage laser rays + periodic Disintegration Beam!
• Sorcerer Mage (T4-B): Curses enemies (halving their armor for 5s) + triple bolt barrage.
💣 Dwarven Bombard (Cost: 125g)

Explosive Area-of-Effect physical damage. Crushes swarms of ground foes stopped by Barracks.

• Big Bertha (T4-A): Massive splash radius + periodic Dragonbreath Cluster Missile.
• Tesla x104 (T4-B): Chain lightning arcs hitting up to 5 Ground & Flying foes!
